# kushantp58.github.io

My personal portfolio site. Built it from scratch — no framework, no build step, just HTML, CSS, and a bit of vanilla JS.

Live at: **https://kushantp58.github.io**

---

## What's in here

```
docs/
├── index.html              # the whole site lives here
├── 404.html                # custom not-found page
├── .nojekyll               # skip Jekyll on GitHub Pages
├── README.md
└── assets/img/
    ├── favicon.svg
    ├── favicon-180.png
    ├── og.svg / og.png     # social share card (1200×630)
    └── photos/             # portrait + field gallery images
```

---

## Sections

1. **Currently** — what I'm working on at Brookhaven National Lab
2. **Experience** — timeline of my roles and education
3. **What I Build** — the three areas I focus on: beamline control, nuclear safeguards, and BCIs
4. **In the Field** — photos from the places I've worked
5. **Selected Papers** — published research I've contributed to
6. **Recognition** — awards and competition results
7. **Writing** — essays and posts I've published
8. **Contact** — resume, email, GitHub, LinkedIn

---

## Deploying

Served from `/docs` on the `gh-pages` branch. Edit files, commit, push — that's it.

---

## If you want to tweak something

- **Resume** — two links in `index.html` (nav + contact), both point to Google Drive. Search `drive.google.com` to find them.
- **Photos** — swap files in `assets/img/photos/` or update the `<img src>` paths directly.
- **Writing links** — currently point to Medium; replace with direct post URLs when ready.
